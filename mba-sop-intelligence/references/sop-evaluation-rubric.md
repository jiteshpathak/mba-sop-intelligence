# SOP Evaluation Rubric (100 Points)

> These are skill-internal evaluation weights, not official institutional weightages.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Authenticity and credibility | 15 |
| Clarity of career trajectory | 10 |
| Why MBA? | 15 |
| Why FMS? (or target school specificity) | 10 |
| Personal differentiation | 15 |
| Leadership and initiative | 10 |
| Intellectual curiosity and analytical thinking | 10 |
| Career goals and ambition | 10 |
| Writing quality and coherence | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Dimension Standards

### 1) Authenticity and credibility (15)
- **Excellent**: specific, defensible claims with grounded reflection.
- **Average**: plausible but partially generic and under-evidenced.
- **Poor**: inflated labels, vague impact, unsupported assertions.
- **Red flags**: dramatic claims without context; inconsistent timeline.

### 2) Career trajectory (10)
- **Excellent**: clear arc from prior choices to future direction.
- **Average**: coherent but shallow transitions.
- **Poor**: disjointed narrative; no rationale for pivots.

### 3) Why MBA (15)
- **Excellent**: concrete capability gaps linked to post-MBA roles.
- **Average**: broad growth motivation.
- **Poor**: status-only or credential-only rationale.

### 4) Why FMS / school fit (10)
- **Excellent**: school-specific fit tied to goals and constraints.
- **Average**: partly specific but mostly generic.
- **Poor**: interchangeable with any B-school.

### 5) Personal differentiation (15)
- **Excellent**: memorable, evidence-backed, non-forced distinctiveness.
- **Average**: mild differentiation.
- **Poor**: common profile presented as unique without proof.

### 6) Leadership and initiative (10)
- **Excellent**: ownership under constraints with measurable outcomes.
- **Average**: participation-heavy examples.
- **Poor**: title-heavy with no demonstrated leadership behavior.

### 7) Intellectual curiosity and analytical thinking (10)
- **Excellent**: nuanced problem framing and learning behavior.
- **Average**: some analytical references, limited depth.
- **Poor**: no evidence of thinking process.

### 8) Career goals and ambition (10)
- **Excellent**: realistic near-term + credible long-term arc.
- **Average**: high-level goals with weak path.
- **Poor**: unrealistic or contradictory goals.

### 9) Writing quality and coherence (5)
- **Excellent**: concise, clean flow, no filler.
- **Average**: readable but repetitive.
- **Poor**: cluttered, unclear, over-polished clichés.

## Authenticity Overlay (separate check)

Classify risk:
- Low: natural voice and verifiable claims
- Medium: polished but somewhat generic
- High: formulaic, consultant/AI-like tone, over-symmetric structure

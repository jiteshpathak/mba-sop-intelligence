# Writing Style Guide for SOP Output

## Target Style

- Human, specific, and reflective.
- Moderate polish, not brochure language.
- Crisp paragraphing and transitions.

## Required Traits

- Concrete examples over abstract claims.
- Reflection over self-praise.
- Plain language over jargon.
- Distinct school-fit paragraph tied to goals.

## Prohibited Traits

- Generic inspirational openings.
- Unverifiable grand claims.
- Forced emotional drama.
- Copy-paste consultant template structures.

## Sentence-Level Rules

- Prefer active voice and direct verbs.
- Keep sentence length varied but mostly short-to-medium.
- Avoid stacking adjectives.
- Keep one idea per sentence where possible.

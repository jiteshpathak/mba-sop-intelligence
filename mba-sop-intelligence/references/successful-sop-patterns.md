# Successful SOP Patterns (Synthesized)

## Recurrent Strong Patterns

1. **Specific opening anchored in action**
   - Starts with a real decision/moment, not abstract ambition.
2. **Tight past-present-future logic**
   - Experiences are interpreted, not merely listed.
3. **Role-to-impact translation**
   - Technical work translated into business outcomes.
4. **Concrete "Why MBA now"**
   - Explicit capability gap and timing rationale.
5. **School-specific fit**
   - Mentions resources/learning environment tied to candidate path.
6. **Calibrated ambition**
   - Ambitious but path-based goals.
7. **Interview-defensible claims**
   - Every claim can be explained with context and outcomes.

## Recurrent Weak Patterns

- Generic hero narrative copied from common essay templates.
- Resume repetition without introspection.
- Overuse of buzzwords (leadership, innovation, disruption) without evidence.
- Why-school paragraph that reads interchangeable across institutions.
- Over-polished style with no personal texture.

## Keep / Cut Heuristic

- **Keep**: concrete events, quantified or observable impact, reflective learning.
- **Cut**: cliches, generic mission statements, unsupported superlatives.

# Authenticity and Ethics Guardrails

## Non-Negotiables

- No fabricated achievements, roles, impact metrics, or motivations.
- No guarantee of shortlist/interview/convert outcome.
- No presentation of anecdotal online advice as official policy.
- No claim of reviewing inaccessible private or login-only content.

## AI-Style Risk Review (Heuristic, not detector claim)

Flag writing as potentially inauthentic when it shows:
- repeated abstract virtue statements
- uniformly polished syntax with no personal texture
- high claim density with low concrete context
- emotionally inflated but fact-light storytelling

## Correction Actions

- Replace abstractions with lived examples.
- Remove unsupported claims.
- Ask user for missing specifics where needed.
- Preserve candidate voice and wording where authentic.

## Copyright and Privacy

- Do not reproduce full copyrighted SOPs from public forums.
- Use synthesized patterns and anonymized extracts only.
- Handle category/gender disclosures only if user or source voluntarily disclosed them.

# Ten Independent Reviewer Personas

These are fictional composites for evaluation diversity, not claims about actual FMS committee members.

## 1) FMS Admissions Traditionalist
- Priority: academic credibility, purpose clarity, fit.
- Bias: rejects ornamental writing and inflated ambition.
- Core question: Why should this candidate get a scarce seat?

## 2) Former MBA Interview Panelist
- Priority: defendability under probing.
- Bias: penalizes vague claims.
- Core question: Can every sentence survive cross-questioning?

## 3) FMS Alumnus Career Strategist
- Priority: practical career logic and employability trajectory.
- Bias: dislikes fuzzy role goals.
- Core question: Is the school choice and career path commercially coherent?

## 4) Behavioral Psychologist
- Priority: self-awareness, resilience, motivation quality.
- Bias: spots performative vulnerability.
- Core question: What does this reveal about the person?

## 5) Elite SOP Editor
- Priority: narrative architecture, specificity, language efficiency.
- Bias: intolerant of cliches and redundancy.
- Core question: Is this truly persuasive writing, not a polished resume?

## 6) GEF Candidate Specialist
- Priority: differentiation among engineer-heavy cohorts.
- Bias: rejects stereotype-driven positioning.
- Core question: What is distinctive about this person specifically?

## 7) Corporate Hiring Manager
- Priority: ownership, execution, business impact.
- Bias: skeptical of title without accountability.
- Core question: Would I trust this person with a complex business problem?

## 8) Skeptical Professor
- Priority: curiosity, logic, intellectual depth.
- Bias: rejects shallow frameworks.
- Core question: Is there evidence of management-learning readiness?

## 9) Brutally Honest Admissions Consultant
- Priority: competitiveness vs top decile applicants.
- Bias: aggressively compares against stronger pools.
- Core question: Why might this lose against better-positioned candidates?

## 10) Devil's Advocate
- Priority: contradictions and rejection rationale.
- Bias: assumes overstatement unless proven.
- Core question: If rejecting this file, what is the strongest argument?

## Blind-Review Protocol

Each reviewer must submit independently before seeing other reviews, including:
- score /100
- 3 strengths
- 3 weaknesses
- rejection triggers
- unsupported claims
- specific improvements
- verdict label
